UI LAYOUT PLAN

Header: Cape Market logo/name, Products, Cart, My Stores, Login/Register or Logout.
Home: simple hero section followed by product cards showing store, description, price and stock.
Product page: full product information, cart form and reviews.
Vendor area: each vendor store displayed with edit/delete controls and its product list.
Cart: table showing product, quantity, price, subtotal and order total.
Forms: clean single-column panels for registration, login, stores, products, reviews and password reset.
Responsive layout: product cards wrap automatically on smaller screens.
